# Tethered Drone vs Traditional Drone for Industrial Use

A tethered drone and a traditional battery-powered drone serve different industrial mission requirements.

Tethered drones receive continuous electrical power from a ground station through a tether cable, making them suitable for long-duration monitoring, emergency response, public safety, infrastructure protection, and fixed-position surveillance.

Traditional drones rely on onboard batteries and provide greater mobility, making them better suited for mapping, surveying, mobile inspections, and missions covering large areas.

## Key Differences

- Flight endurance
- Operational range
- Power supply
- Operational safety
- Deployment speed
- Operating costs
- Best industrial applications

## Flight Endurance

Traditional battery-powered drones generally operate for a limited period before they must return for battery replacement or charging.

Tethered drones receive continuous power from the ground, allowing significantly longer operating times for persistent missions.

## Operational Range

Traditional drones can move freely across large areas and are suitable for inspections, mapping, and surveying.

Tethered drones operate within the radius and altitude permitted by the tether cable, making them more suitable for fixed-position monitoring.

## Power Architecture

Traditional drones must share onboard battery capacity between propulsion, cameras, sensors, communication systems, and other payloads.

Tethered drones receive continuous electrical power from a ground station, supporting longer operation of power-intensive payloads.

## Industrial Applications

Tethered drones are commonly used for:

- Emergency response
- Public safety
- Perimeter security
- Infrastructure monitoring
- Event surveillance
- Communication relay

Traditional drones are commonly used for:

- Mapping
- Surveying
- Powerline inspection
- Pipeline inspection
- Construction monitoring
- Agriculture

## Choosing the Right Platform

Choose a tethered drone when continuous operation and persistent observation are the main priorities.

Choose a traditional drone when mobility, long-distance movement, and rapid deployment across multiple locations are more important.
