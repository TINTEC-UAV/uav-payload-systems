# 7 Real-World Tethered Drone Applications

![7 Real-World Tethered Drone Applications](../images/Industrial tethered drone applications cover.webp)

Tethered drone systems provide continuous electrical power to an airborne UAV through a lightweight cable connected to a ground power station.

Unlike conventional battery-powered drones, tethered UAVs can maintain persistent aerial coverage without frequent landing and battery replacement.

## Key Tethered Drone Applications

### 1. Oil and Gas Monitoring

Tethered drones can support continuous observation of refineries, pipelines, storage facilities and other critical oil and gas infrastructure.

### 2. Power Utility Inspection

Applications include transmission-line monitoring, substation observation, storm-damage assessment and maintenance-site supervision.

### 3. Construction Monitoring

A persistent aerial platform can help monitor bridge construction, high-rise projects, equipment movement and site safety.

### 4. Mining Operations

Tethered drones can provide real-time views of open-pit mines, active work zones, heavy equipment and restricted areas.

### 5. Public Safety and Emergency Response

Emergency teams can deploy tethered drones with visible-light cameras, thermal cameras, searchlights or communication payloads.

### 6. Temporary Communications

A tethered UAV can carry communications equipment to provide elevated temporary network coverage during events, emergencies or remote operations.

### 7. Event and Perimeter Security

Persistent aerial monitoring can support crowd management, perimeter observation and temporary security operations.

## Why Use a Tethered Drone?

The main advantages include:

- Extended airborne operation
- Continuous ground-based power
- Persistent monitoring
- Stable coverage over a designated area
- Support for cameras, lighting and communication payloads
- Reduced interruption from battery replacement

## Tethered Drone vs. Battery-Powered Drone

Tethered drones are best suited for long-duration operation over a fixed area.

Battery-powered drones remain more appropriate for route inspection, mapping, long-distance movement and missions that require unrestricted mobility.
